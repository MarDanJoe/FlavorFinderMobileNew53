Support

Last updated September 30, 2026 · Flavor Finder

Contact Flavor Finder for account help, privacy requests or problems using the app.

Getting help

Email the support address below with your app version, iOS version, what happened and the steps leading to the issue. Never include a password, verification code, API key or payment credential.

Location and restaurant results

If location permission is unavailable, choose a city or ZIP code instead. Check your connection when restaurant information cannot refresh. Google Maps photos and reviews include links to their original content where supplied; use those links to report provider content.

Recovery and deletion

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen to request a recovery code. Use Profile → Delete account to delete a registered account after reauthentication. Email support if you cannot access the account or need help with data deletion.
