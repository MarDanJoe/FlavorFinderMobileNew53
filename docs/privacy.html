Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026 · Flavor Finder

Flavor Finder helps you discover places to eat and choose together with friends. This policy describes the current US service and how we handle information.

Location and restaurant discovery

With your permission, we use your device location to find nearby restaurants. You can instead enter a city or ZIP code. Search coordinates and search text pass through our hosted restaurant service to Google Maps Platform. We do not keep a database of your location history. Providers may process request metadata, IP addresses and technical logs for service operation and security.

Accounts and saved places

An account is optional. Supabase processes your email, password, display name and authentication activity for sign-in, confirmation and recovery. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored in plaintext by Flavor Finder. Authentication sessions are stored in the iPhone keychain or browser storage. Registered users’ saved Google place IDs and discovery preferences are stored for synchronization. Guest favorites and preferences stay on the device; guests receive an anonymous authentication identity for restaurant requests and group participation.

Voting with friends

Rooms store restaurant IDs, a room code, host and member identities, nicknames, votes, progress and the final aggregate scores and winner. People with the code can join while the lobby is open. Members see nicknames and progress; each person can access their own individual votes. Members see aggregate results after voting ends. Small groups may infer individual preferences from totals. Share room codes only with people you intend to invite.

Our service providers

Google Maps Platform supplies restaurant information, photos and reviews and processes restaurant queries. Supabase hosts authentication, database records and backend functions. Brevo delivers account confirmation and recovery emails and processes sender and recipient addresses, message content and delivery events. Email activity may include open events when a mail client loads an embedded tracking image. These providers process data under their service terms and privacy policies, and may process it outside your state or country.

Retention and deletion

Saved place IDs, preferences and account records remain until you remove them or delete the account. Room access expires after 24 hours. An hourly cleanup normally deletes room records, memberships and votes within 25 hours of room creation; service pauses or scheduling delays can delay physical deletion. Deleting an account removes its synced library, memberships, individual votes and rooms it hosted. Aggregate scores in another host’s completed room remain until room cleanup. Technical logs, backups and email delivery records may remain under provider retention settings or where required for security or legal obligations; immediate erasure from all provider systems is not guaranteed.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can decline location permission and search by city, remove saved places, and use Profile → Delete account to delete a registered account after reauthentication. Clearing app data removes local guest favorites and preferences but does not itself delete hosted guest identities or room records. Contact our support email to request access, correction or deletion of personal information or help with a guest identity; we may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Do not email passwords or verification codes.

Advertising, purchases and children

The current app has no advertising SDK, paid membership, advertising identifier collection or cross-app advertising tracking. If we introduce advertising or payments, we will update the policy and required permissions before enabling them. Flavor Finder is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Security and policy updates

We use access controls, encrypted network connections and platform credential storage to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. We will update this policy when our practices change and provide any notice required for material changes. Contact Flavor Finder at the support email below for privacy questions.
